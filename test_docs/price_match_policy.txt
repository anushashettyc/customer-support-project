OMNISUPPORT AI - PRICE MATCH & ADJUSTMENT POLICY
=================================================

We Want You to Get the Best Price!

1. PRICE MATCH GUARANTEE
------------------------

We will match competitors' prices on identical items:

Eligible competitors:
- Amazon.com
- BestBuy.com
- Walmart.com
- Target.com
- Costco.com (members only)
- Authorized retailer websites

Requirements:
- Item must be identical (same brand, model, color, size)
- Item must be in stock at competitor
- Price must be current (not expired deal)
- Must be sold and shipped by competitor (not third-party)
- Excludes marketplace sellers

How to request price match:
1. Find lower price at eligible competitor
2. Contact us via chat, phone, or email
3. Provide competitor URL showing lower price
4. We verify and adjust within 24 hours

Price match is applied as:
- Pre-purchase: Adjusted price at checkout
- Post-purchase: Refund of difference to original payment

2. POST-PURCHASE PRICE ADJUSTMENTS
----------------------------------

If our price drops after you purchase:

Adjustment windows by tier:
- Bronze members: 7 days
- Silver members: 14 days
- Gold members: 21 days
- Platinum members: 30 days

How to request adjustment:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to Order History
3. Click "Request Price Adjustment"
4. Automatic refund within 3-5 business days

OR contact support with order number

Adjustment limits:
- One adjustment per item per order
- Maximum 3 adjustments per customer per month
- Excludes clearance, flash sales, and limited promotions
- Excludes Black Friday / Cyber Monday prices

3. EXCLUSIONS
-------------

Price match/adjustment NOT available for:
- Clearance or open-box items
- Bundle deals (match individual items only)
- Membership club prices (Costco, Sam's Club)
- Flash sales or limited-time deals under 24 hours
- Pricing errors (competitor or ours)
- Out of stock items
- Refurbished products
- Gift cards
- Services and warranties
- Marketplace or third-party sellers
- Auction sites (eBay, etc.)
- International pricing
- Coupon stacking deals

4. COMPETITOR COUPONS
---------------------

We DO NOT match:
- Competitor-specific coupons
- Credit card rewards pricing
- Employee discounts
- Student/military discounts at competitors

We DO match:
- Publicly available sale prices
- Advertised doorbuster deals (while in stock)
- Clearance prices at eligible competitors

5. BEST PRICE GUARANTEE
-----------------------

For Platinum members only:

Automatic price protection:
- We monitor prices for 30 days after purchase
- If price drops, refund issued automatically
- No action needed from customer
- Notification sent when adjustment made

Price match plus:
- We match competitor price AND beat by 5%
- Maximum additional discount: $50
- Valid once per item

6. HOW ADJUSTMENTS ARE REFUNDED
-------------------------------

Refund method matches original payment:
- Credit card: 3-5 business days
- Debit card: 5-7 business days
- PayPal: 1-3 business days
- Store credit: Immediate
- Gift card: Added to account balance

For large adjustments (over $100):
- May require manager approval
- Processed within 48 hours
- Customer notified of status

7. SPECIAL SITUATIONS
---------------------

Items purchased during sale:
- Can still request adjustment if price drops further
- Original sale price is your baseline, not regular price

Multiple quantity purchases:
- Adjustment applies to all identical items in order
- Example: Bought 3 items at $50, price drops to $45
  → Receive $15 refund ($5 x 3)

Items with rebates:
- Price match based on pre-rebate price
- Rebates handled separately

Price match on bundles:
- We can match bundle if identical bundle exists
- OR match individual component prices and apply
- Whichever is more favorable to customer

8. ABUSE PREVENTION
-------------------

We reserve the right to deny price adjustments for:
- Suspected fraud or abuse
- Excessive adjustment requests (>5 per month)
- Purchases clearly made to exploit pricing
- Manipulation of competitor pricing

Consequences of abuse:
- First offense: Warning
- Second offense: 90-day suspension of adjustment privileges
- Third offense: Permanent ban from price adjustments

9. TIMING CONSIDERATIONS
------------------------

Best times to buy:
- Major sales: Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Prime Day
- End of season clearance
- New model release (old model discounts)
- Holiday weekends

Price drop patterns:
- Electronics: Prices drop when new models announced
- Seasonal items: 50-70% off end of season
- TVs: Best prices in January and May
