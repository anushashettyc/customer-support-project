4. CUSTOMER SUPPORT PRIORITY
----------------------------
Support queue priority based on tier:

   Platinum: IMMEDIATE - Jump to front of queue, dedicated line
   Gold: HIGH PRIORITY - Average wait time under 5 minutes
   Silver: MEDIUM PRIORITY - Average wait time under 15 minutes
   Bronze: STANDARD - Average wait time under 30 minutes

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION PRIORITY
------------------------------
When resolving customer complaints and disputes:

   Platinum Customers:
   - Benefit of the doubt given in ambiguous situations
   - Higher refund limits without manager approval
   - Goodwill gestures more readily offered
   - Exceptions made for minor policy violations

   Gold Customers:
   - Favorable consideration in dispute resolution
   - Moderate flexibility on policy enforcement
   - Eligible for goodwill gestures

   Silver/Bronze Customers:
   - Standard policy enforcement
   - Exceptions require manager approval

6. TIER ADVANCEMENT
-------------------
   - Tier status is calculated based on rolling 12-month spending
   - Status updates on the 1st of each month
   - Tier upgrades take effect immediately upon qualification
   - Tier downgrades occur after 12 months below threshold
   - 3-month grace period provided before downgrade

7. TIER MAINTENANCE
-------------------
To maintain your current tier:
   - Bronze: No minimum (default tier)
   - Silver: Spend at least $500 in any 12-month period
   - Gold: Spend at least $1,500 in any 12-month period
   - Platinum: Spend at least $5,000 in any 12-month period

8. SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR VIP CUSTOMERS
---------------------------------------
Our Platinum (VIP) customers receive special consideration:

   a) RETURN FLEXIBILITY
      - Returns accepted up to 60 days (double standard window)
      - Return condition requirements relaxed for minor use
      - Free return shipping on all returns
      - No restocking fees under any circumstances

   b) REFUND PREFERENCE
      - Full refunds preferred over store credit
      - Expedited refund processing (2-3 business days)
      - Goodwill refunds available for extenuating circumstances

   c) DAMAGE CLAIMS
      - Extended reporting window (14 days vs 7 days standard)
      - Photo evidence not always required for small claims
      - Replacement shipped before return received

   d) WARRANTY CLAIMS
      - Extended warranty consideration (+30 days)
      - Expedited claim processing
      - Loaner products available during repair

9. EARNING POINTS
-----------------
In addition to tier benefits, members earn points:
   - 1 point per $1 spent
   - Bonus points during promotional periods
   - 100 points = $5 store credit
   - Points expire 12 months after earning
