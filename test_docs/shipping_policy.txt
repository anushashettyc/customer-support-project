OMNISUPPORT AI - SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
============================================

Last Updated: October 2024

1. SHIPPING METHODS & TIMEFRAMES
--------------------------------

Standard Shipping:
- Delivery: 5-7 business days
- Cost: Free for orders over $50, otherwise $5.99
- Tracking: Provided via email within 24 hours

Express Shipping:
- Delivery: 2-3 business days
- Cost: $12.99 for all orders
- Tracking: Real-time tracking provided

Overnight Shipping:
- Delivery: Next business day (order by 2 PM local time)
- Cost: $24.99 for all orders
- Tracking: Real-time tracking with signature confirmation

2. SHIPPING DESTINATIONS
------------------------

Domestic (United States):
- We ship to all 50 states
- PO Boxes accepted for Standard shipping only
- APO/FPO addresses: 7-14 business days

International:
- Currently shipping to Canada, UK, Australia, and EU countries
- Delivery: 10-21 business days
- Customer responsible for customs duties and taxes
- Some products may have shipping restrictions

3. ORDER PROCESSING
-------------------

- Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same day
- Orders placed after 2 PM EST ship next business day
- Weekend orders processed on Monday
- Order confirmation email sent within 1 hour

4. DELIVERY ISSUES
------------------

Lost Packages:
- Report within 7 days of expected delivery
- We will investigate with carrier (5-10 business days)
- Full refund or replacement if package confirmed lost
- Platinum customers: Immediate replacement shipped

Damaged During Shipping:
- Report within 48 hours of delivery
- Photo evidence required
- Full replacement shipped within 24 hours
- No need to return damaged item for orders under $100

Wrong Item Received:
- Report within 7 days of delivery
- Correct item shipped immediately
- Prepaid return label provided for wrong item
- 10% discount on next order as apology

Delayed Shipments:
- If delivery exceeds estimated time by 3+ days:
  - Standard customers: $10 store credit
  - Silver customers: $15 store credit
  - Gold customers: $25 store credit + free upgrade to Express
  - Platinum customers: Full shipping refund + $50 credit

5. SIGNATURE REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------

Signature required for:
- Orders over $200
- Electronics and high-value items
- Overnight shipments
- International orders

If no one available to sign:
- Carrier will attempt delivery 3 times
- Package held at local facility for 7 days
- Customer can authorize release without signature (at own risk)

6. TRACKING YOUR ORDER
----------------------

Track your order:
- Via order confirmation email link
- On our website under "My Orders"
- By contacting customer support with order number

Tracking updates:
- "Label Created": Order processed, awaiting carrier pickup
- "In Transit": Package moving through carrier network
- "Out for Delivery": Arriving today
- "Delivered": Package delivered (check porch, mailroom, neighbors)

7. SPECIAL HANDLING
-------------------

Fragile Items:
- Extra packaging and "Fragile" labels applied
- No additional charge
- Recommend Express shipping for best handling

Oversized Items:
- Furniture and large items: 7-14 business days
- White glove delivery available: $49.99
- Includes inside delivery and basic assembly

Hazardous Materials:
- Batteries shipped ground only
- Certain chemicals cannot be shipped
- Restrictions apply to international orders

8. HOLIDAY SHIPPING
-------------------

During peak seasons (Nov 15 - Dec 31):
- Order 2 days earlier than normal for guaranteed delivery
- Express and Overnight cutoff times move to 12 PM
- Additional $3 surcharge may apply to some routes

Holiday shipping deadlines provided annually on website.
