OMNISUPPORT AI - PRODUCT CARE & MAINTENANCE GUIDE
==================================================

Keep Your Products in Perfect Condition

SECTION 1: ELECTRONICS CARE
---------------------------

Smartphones & Tablets:
- Clean screen with microfiber cloth only
- Avoid extreme temperatures (-4°F to 113°F operating range)
- Remove case monthly to clean trapped debris
- Keep away from water (even water-resistant devices)
- Use original or certified chargers only
- Battery health: Keep between 20-80% charge for longevity
- Update software regularly for security and performance

Laptops & Computers:
- Shut down weekly to clear memory and apply updates
- Clean keyboard with compressed air monthly
- Wipe screen with electronics-safe cleaner
- Ensure ventilation - don't block air vents
- Use surge protector always
- Defragment HDD quarterly (not needed for SSD)
- Backup data weekly to cloud or external drive

Smart Watches:
- Rinse with fresh water after swimming or sweating
- Dry thoroughly before charging
- Clean sensors on back with soft cloth
- Remove band monthly to clean underneath
- Avoid soap, lotion, and harsh chemicals
- Replace band every 12-18 months for hygiene

Headphones & Earbuds:
- Wipe down after each use
- Clean ear tips weekly with isopropyl alcohol
- Store in case when not in use
- Avoid leaving in hot car
- Don't share earbuds (hygiene)
- Replace ear tips every 6 months

SECTION 2: APPLIANCE CARE
-------------------------

Kitchen Appliances:

Coffee Makers:
- Descale monthly with white vinegar solution
- Clean carafe after each use
- Replace water filter every 2 months
- Wipe exterior weekly
- Empty grounds immediately after brewing

Blenders:
- Rinse immediately after use
- Deep clean: blend warm water + dish soap
- Check blade tightness monthly
- Replace gasket annually
- Don't overfill past max line

Air Fryers:
- Clean basket after every use
- Wipe heating element monthly (when cool)
- Don't use metal utensils inside
- Check for food buildup in vents
- Deep clean interior monthly

Microwaves:
- Clean spills immediately
- Steam clean weekly: microwave water + lemon 3 min
- Clean door seals monthly
- Don't run empty
- Replace if door doesn't seal properly

Home Appliances:

Vacuum Cleaners:
- Empty canister/replace bag when 2/3 full
- Clean filters monthly
- Check brush roll for hair/debris weekly
- Replace HEPA filter annually
- Inspect belt every 6 months

Air Purifiers:
- Replace filters per manufacturer schedule
- Clean pre-filter monthly
- Wipe exterior weekly
- Don't block intake/output vents
- Run 24/7 for best results (energy efficient)

Robot Vacuums:
- Empty dustbin after each run
- Clean sensors weekly
- Remove hair from brushes weekly
- Clean charging contacts monthly
- Replace brushes every 6-12 months

SECTION 4: TROUBLESHOOTING BEFORE CONTACTING SUPPORT
----------------------------------------------------

Electronics Not Turning On:
1. Check if charged (charge 30 min minimum)
2. Try different outlet/charger
3. Perform hard reset (hold power 15-30 seconds)
4. Check for physical damage
5. Contact support if still not working

Device Running Slowly:
1. Restart device
2. Check storage space (need 10%+ free)
3. Close unused apps
4. Check for software updates
5. Factory reset as last resort (backup first!)

Connectivity Issues:
1. Toggle WiFi/Bluetooth off and on
2. Restart device
3. Restart router/modem
4. Forget network and reconnect
5. Check for interference from other devices

Battery Draining Fast:
1. Check battery health in settings
2. Reduce screen brightness
3. Disable unused features (GPS, Bluetooth)
4. Check for apps using excessive power
5. Consider battery replacement if >2 years old

SECTION 5: WHEN TO SEEK PROFESSIONAL REPAIR
-------------------------------------------

Seek repair immediately if:
- Smoke, burning smell, or unusual heat
- Cracked battery or swelling
- Exposed wires or components
- Sparking when charging
- Water damage with visible corrosion

Don't attempt self-repair on:
- Sealed batteries
- Screens with digitizer
- Motherboards or logic boards
- Charging ports
- Speakers with water damage

SECTION 6: PRODUCT LIFESPAN EXPECTATIONS
----------------------------------------

Typical lifespan with proper care:
- Smartphones: 3-4 years
- Laptops: 4-6 years
- Tablets: 4-5 years
- Smart watches: 3-4 years
- Wireless earbuds: 2-3 years
- Kitchen appliances: 8-12 years
- Vacuum cleaners: 8-10 years
- Air purifiers: 5-8 years

Signs it's time to replace:
- Repair cost exceeds 50% of replacement cost
- Multiple recurring issues
- No longer receiving software/security updates
- Battery replacement not cost effective
- Performance significantly degraded
