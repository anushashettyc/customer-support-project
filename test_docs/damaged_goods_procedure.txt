                    OMNISUPPORT RETAIL - DAMAGED GOODS PROCEDURE
                         Guidelines for Reporting & Resolution
                         Effective Date: January 1, 2024
================================================================================

1. TYPES OF DAMAGE COVERED
--------------------------
This procedure covers items that arrive in damaged condition due to:

   a) SHIPPING DAMAGE - Caused during transit
      - Crushed or dented packaging
      - Broken seals or torn boxes
      - Water damage from exposure
      - Impact damage (drops, throws)

   b) MANUFACTURING DEFECTS - Present before shipping
      - Cracked, chipped, or broken components
      - Missing parts or accessories
      - Cosmetic defects (scratches, dents, discoloration)
      - Non-functional features

   c) PACKAGING DEFECTS
      - Insufficient protection
      - Wrong packaging for item type
      - Missing protective materials

3. HOW TO REPORT DAMAGE
-----------------------
   Step 1: DO NOT DISCARD PACKAGING
           Keep all boxes, packing materials, and damaged items

   Step 2: DOCUMENT THE DAMAGE
           Take clear photos showing:
           - Overall condition of package upon arrival
           - Damage to outer packaging
           - Damage to inner packaging/protective materials
           - Damage to the product itself
           - Close-up shots of specific damage areas
           - Shipping label (for carrier claims)

   Step 3: CONTACT CUSTOMER SUPPORT
           Methods:
           - Online: Submit damage claim form at omnisupport.com/damage
           - Email: damage@omnisupport.com
           - Phone: 1-800-OMNI-HELP (select option 2)
           - Chat: Live chat on website or app

   Step 4: PROVIDE REQUIRED INFORMATION
           - Order ID
           - Product name and SKU
           - Date of delivery
           - Description of damage
           - Photos (attach to claim)
           - Preferred resolution (refund or replacement)

4. EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
   REQUIRED for all damage claims:
   - Order ID or proof of purchase
   - Description of damage
   - At least 2 photos showing damage

   RECOMMENDED for faster processing:
   - Photo of shipping label
   - Photo of outer box condition
   - Video showing defect (for functional issues)
   - Screenshot of delivery notification

   WAIVED for VIP (Platinum) customers on claims under $100

5. RESOLUTION PROCESS
---------------------
   Day 1: Damage report received and logged
   Day 1-2: Initial review by customer support team
   Day 2-3: Assessment and determination
   Day 3-5: Resolution communicated to customer
   Day 5-7: Refund processed or replacement shipped

   EXPEDITED for VIP customers: 1-3 days total

6. RESOLUTION OPTIONS
---------------------

   A. FULL REFUND
   - Original purchase price returned
   - Taxes returned
   - Shipping costs returned
   - Processed to original payment method

   B. REPLACEMENT
   - Identical item shipped free of charge
   - Express shipping at no extra cost
   - Original damaged item may need to be returned
   - If item out of stock: refund or equivalent item offered

   C. PARTIAL REFUND
   - Offered if damage is minor/cosmetic
   - Customer keeps damaged item at reduced price
   - Discount typically 20-50% based on damage severity

   D. REPAIR
   - Available for certain product categories
   - Free repair service arranged
   - Loaner item may be provided during repair (VIP only)

7. DISPOSAL OF DAMAGED ITEMS
----------------------------
   Based on item value and damage type:

   Items UNDER $50:
   - Customer may dispose of item
   - No return required
   - Refund/replacement processed immediately

   Items $50-$200:
   - Photos sufficient in most cases
   - Return may be required for investigation
   - Prepaid return label provided if needed

   Items OVER $200:
   - Return generally required
   - Pickup may be arranged for large items
   - Inspection conducted upon receipt
   - Refund processed after inspection (3-5 days)

   HAZARDOUS ITEMS:
   - Do not return - special disposal instructions provided
   - Refund/replacement processed without return

8. CARRIER DAMAGE CLAIMS
------------------------
For severe shipping damage, we may file a carrier claim:
   - Customer cooperates with carrier inspection if requested
   - Does not delay customer refund/replacement
   - We handle all carrier communication

9. FRAUD PREVENTION
-------------------
To protect against fraudulent damage claims:
   - Photo metadata may be verified
   - Delivery photos from carrier reviewed
   - Pattern analysis on customer account
   - Reserve right to require return before refund

   Consequences of fraudulent claims:
   - Claim denied
   - Account suspended
   - Legal action in severe cases

10. SPECIAL CASES
-----------------
    CONCEALED DAMAGE (discovered after unpacking):
    - Report within 48 hours of discovery
    - Document how item was stored before discovery
    - Full resolution options available

    PARTIAL SHIPMENT DAMAGE:
    - Only damaged items need to be reported
    - Undamaged items from same order not affected

    GIFT RECIPIENTS:
    - Can report damage with gift receipt
    - Store credit issued to recipient
    - Refund to original purchaser available upon request
